# Curry Mustard Vinaigrette

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup rice wine vinegar
  - I use unsweetened, but the sweetened stuff tastes great
  - white wine vinegar would work as well
- 1/4 cup dijon mustard
  - you may optionally *add* or *substitute* brown mustard and/or whole grain mustard
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 1 tbsp honey
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a bottle and shake

## Notes
I don't bother to refrigerate this. The olive oil solidifies and I find that annoying to deal with.
Just be sure to use it in a week or two.
