# Masoor Daal

## Ingredients
- 300g red lentils, soaked 15m and rinsed
- 1L water
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- garlic/ginger paste
- 2 tsp jeera (cumin seeds)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander (optional)
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 2 green chiles
- 2 tbsp ghee
- fresh coriander and/or spinach (optional)

## Instructions
1. Soak the lentils and rinse thoroughly
2. Dice the onion and tomatoes
3. Heat the ghee in a dutch oven or pot/pan that has a cover on medium/low heat
4. Add the jeera and lightly fry for about 1 minute, taking care not to burn it
5. Add the diced onion and garlic/ginger paste, and turn up the heat a bit. Cook until onions are soft and slightly browned, about 5 minutes
6. Add the salt, turmeric, red chilli powder, and green chiles. Stir while cooking for 3-4 minutes, taking care not to burn the turmeric. Turn heat down if necessary.
7. Add the diced tomato and cook until soft and you have a nice paste going
8. Add the rinsed lentils and cook for 3-4 minutes while stirring
9. Add 1L of water, bring to a gentle boil
10. Turn to low, cover, and cook for 20 minutes. Take care to stir frequently and prevent any sticking to the pot.
11. Remove cover. Cook a bit longer if too watery.
12. (Optional) Stir in some fresh spinach
13. (Optional) Garnish with fresh chopped coriander
